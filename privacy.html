Privacy Policy

TheBestBits Poster is a private tool used only by the owner of the TheBestBits accounts to publish our own videos to our own TikTok account.

What we access: Only the basic profile information TikTok provides when the owner logs in (user.info.basic) and permission to upload and publish videos to that same account (video.upload, video.publish).

What we store: An access token for the owner's own account, saved privately on the owner's computer. No information about other TikTok users is collected, stored, sold or shared.

How it is used: Only to post the owner's own videos.

Removal: The owner can disconnect the tool at any time in the TikTok app (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions).
